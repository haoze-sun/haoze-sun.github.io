Biography

I am an incoming PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of British Columbia, supervised by Dr. Renjie Liao. I received my MPhil in Electronic and Information Engineering from Tsinghua University, supervised by Dr. Yujiu Yang, and my B.E. in Electronic Information Engineering from Nanjing University of Post and Telecommunications.

My research interests include embodied intelligence and spatial intelligence, with a focus on world action models, vision-language-action models, and enabling vision-language models with spatial understanding capabilities.
